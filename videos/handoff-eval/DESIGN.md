# Design

1920 × 1080, 50 seconds, silent and caption-first for LinkedIn. Forest green and warm ivory. Montserrat 900 carries the argument; IBM Plex Mono 400 carries measurements. Both are embedded by HyperFrames. Large copy, mint accents, amber for blocked actions.

Actual AgentWorld recording and persisted evidence are the proof. No fabricated state or model messages. Results labelled scripted baseline. Motion rules: spring-pop-entrance and stat-bars-and-fills. Catalog component animated-bar-chart adapted to three measured bars with a zero baseline; decorative curve and sample statistics removed.

Four sub-compositions, deterministic timelines, local media. User requested autonomous completion of v1; preview and render proceed without further review gates.
